# Feature request: Sri Lankan commerce and repair workflow

- Use Sri Lankan rupees (LKR) as the default currency and keep USD as an option.
- Tax and service charge are optional and default to zero.
- Prefer a future Discord webhook notification channel instead of email notifications.
- A Windows installation or other service selected in Sales & Services must create a trackable repair ticket.
- Track service work through pending, in progress, waiting for the customer, delivered, and completed states.
- Add a customer WhatsApp phone number to service intake.
- Provide a WhatsApp contact button that opens a pre-filled job-status message; make the templates editable in Settings.
- Make the sales grid much denser and provide a tile-size slider.
- Record actual cost separately from selling price.
- Treat services as zero-cost by default and calculate product/service profit in reports.
