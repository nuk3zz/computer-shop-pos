# Catalog supplier link and supplier transaction evidence

User requirements received 2026-09-29:

- Creating a physical catalog item may optionally record that its opening stock was purchased from a selected supplier.
- Record quantity, unit cost, invoice/reference, and whether payment was full, partial, or zero.
- A supplier that does not allow credit must force full payment; the owner cannot uncheck it.
- A credit supplier may receive a partial initial payment and carry the remaining balance as debt.
- The purchase and every later payment must appear as separate entries with date and time in a complete supplier transaction history.
- Initial and later payments may optionally attach an invoice, receipt, or screenshot. Leaving the attachment empty is valid.
